The Problem

30 seconds of audio is enough to clone a voice.

Generative AI has made voice cloning accessible to anyone. A scammer who records 30 seconds of a family member's speech can produce a synthetic voice that sounds convincingly real on a phone call. The person being targeted has no way to know.

Existing call-screening tools work on transcripts and caller ID. Transcripts are useless for detecting a fake voice - the acoustic giveaways are destroyed by speech-to-text. Caller ID is trivially spoofed.

!

The generalisation gap is the project. Lab error rate 2.85% becomes approximately 35.24% on real-world multilingual audio. Phone compression destroys the detail the models rely on. Training through that compression is how we close it.

How It Works

Four AI checks. Out-of-band. In parallel.

Analysis runs on a copy of the audio stream. The call audio does not pass through any model on its way to the listener. A model failure degrades to no warning, never a dropped or altered call.

W
Machine Fingerprints
XLS-R + AASIST-L (~85K params, CPU)
Synthesized waveform artifacts and acoustic patterns that exist only in AI-generated speech. Multilingual: Hindi, English, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali and more.
P
Speaker Identity
ECAPA-TDNN
Voiceprint matching. Three outcomes (Round 2): no match, match-synthetic (clone of enrolled person - the dangerous case), match-human. Round 1 returns no_enrolment as a neutral state.
R
Rhythm & Pitch
openSMILE
Prosodic features: jitter, shimmer, pitch contour irregularities. AI voices often show subtle unnaturalness in rhythm and micro-timing.
S
Script Analysis
Whisper + SLM
In-memory transcript - discarded immediately - checked for coercion patterns, urgency language, and known scam structures. Transcript never leaves the check boundary.
Architecture

Seven stages. Under 400ms to your screen.

The pipeline processes audio from call ingestion to on-screen warning in under 400ms p90. A virtual-number bridge adds roughly 100 to 330ms on top.

Stage 01-02
Call Source & Audio Ingestion
Exotel primary (call audio streamed to backend while the call continues normally). WebRTC fallback with identical backend. Codec handling: Opus / G.711 / AMR. 20ms frames.
Stage 03
Live Pipeline
WebSocket, Redis Streams short buffer, silence filtering.
under 20ms
Stage 04
Four AI Checks (Parallel, on a Copy)
Machine fingerprints, speaker identity, prosody, script analysis run simultaneously on a copy of the audio. A straggler is marked FAILED, never a verdict.
under 180ms
Stage 05
Risk Fusion
AI signals combined with number reputation, location, time of day, and call history into a 0-100 score. Score updates roughly once per second. Policy rules are per deployment.
under 30ms
Stage 06
Response
Warning overlay drawn over whatever app is in the foreground. Configurable: SMS, stop payment, force callback, second factor.
under 50ms
Stage 07
Evidence
Alert fingerprint sealed in a Merkle tree. Root published where it cannot be quietly rewritten. No audio, no transcript, no voice data on ledger - only tamper-evidence anchors. Handed to NCRP / 1930 on request.
